Questions
Is booking really free?

Yes. You pay the arena entry only on launch day, if you want to play.

Is this real money trading?

No. You trade a demo MT5 account – your entry fee is the only money at risk. Prizes come from 80% of the entries in your arena.

Why “if 100 play”?

There is no fixed prize. The ladder shows what ranks 1–20 get if all 100 seats play. Real prizes are 80% of the real entries.

Do I earn for inviting friends?

Yes. After booking you get your own invite link. Every friend who books with it stays linked to you – when they play paid battles, you get 1% of each entry they pay, forever, in your PipClash wallet. Nothing is paid for bookings alone.

What if an arena is full?

You join the waitlist. Booked seats not used by 4 PM on launch day go to the waitlist in order.

How many trades do I need to win?

In paid battles you must open at least 3 trades during the battle and end in profit to win a prize. One lucky trade is not enough.

What if no one ends in profit?

No one wins a prize, and every trader gets 80% of their entry fee back in their wallet the same evening ($8 on a $10 entry). If even one trader is in profit, the whole 80% pool goes to the traders in profit.

What if fewer than 5 players join?

That battle is cancelled and every entry is refunded in full.
