How prizes are paid

Results are checked for two hours after the battle and are final at 9:30 PM UAE. Then we contact each winner – on Telegram, or by the email on your website account – to collect payment details. Before paying we check a photo ID (passport, national ID or Emirates ID) – the name and phone must match the entry. One prize per person per day. Disqualified players are not ranked.
