Rules and FAQ

01

Entry is $10, $25 or $50

Pick a table ($10, $25 or $50) and pay from your PipClash wallet. 80% of that table’s entry fees go to its winners, 20% is our platform fee. There is no fixed prize – the more traders join, the bigger the prizes. You can cancel your entry for a full refund until you are added to the battle. Fewer than 5 traders on a table by 4:00 PM UAE = that battle is cancelled and every fee goes back to your wallet.

02

One person, one entry

Each entry is tied to one phone number, on the website or on Telegram. A second entry from the same person is removed.

03

Everyone in your table gets the same account

A fresh MT5 account – $10 table → $1,000 · $25 table → $2,500 · $50 table → $5,000 – with 1:500 leverage, sent to you at 4:25 PM UAE. It is used for this battle only.

04

Trade only in the battle window

Open trades only between 4:30 PM and 7:30 PM UAE. At the start your account must be untouched: your table's full balance ($1,000 / $2,500 / $5,000) and no open trades.

05

All trades close at the end

Every position is closed automatically. Your result is calculated at the exact end-time price.

06

Highest profit wins

Rank is your profit from trades opened during the battle. To win a prize you must finish above your starting balance with at least 3 trades. A trade counts only if it stays open at least 1 minute (very short trades still count in your profit, just not in the 3). At 7 PM UAE our Telegram bot reminds traders in profit who have fewer than 3. If only one or a few traders qualify, the whole 80% pool is shared among them. If no one qualifies, every trader gets 80% of their entry fee back in their wallet.

07

Results are checked

Every result is reviewed for two hours. The final result is published at 9:30 PM UAE and the admin’s decision is final.

08

Prizes

Each table ($10, $25, $50) has its own prize pool: 80% of that table’s entry fees. Up to the top 20% of traders win (at least 3 prizes when the pool allows – every prize is at least $10, so a small pool pays fewer winners). Only traders who finish above their starting balance (the same for everyone in that table: $1,000 / $2,500 / $5,000) with at least 3 trades can win. If fewer traders qualify, the whole 80% pool is shared among them – one qualifier takes it all. If nobody qualifies, every entrant gets 80% of their entry fee back. The exact prize for each rank is shown on the Live Arena once entry closes at 4:00 PM UAE. Equal profit = those prizes are shared equally. One prize per person per day. Prizes go to your PipClash wallet; a photo ID check is needed before you withdraw.

You will be disqualified if you

trade before the start or keep a trade open from before
change your MT5 password
add or withdraw demo balance
use robots, EAs, copy trading or trade bots
open opposite trades across two accounts (hedging)
enter more than once with different numbers

Questions

Is this real money?

Trading happens on a demo account with virtual money, so your trades never use real money. The entry fee ($10, $25 or $50) is real money and is not returned if you do not win (except when a battle is cancelled, or 80% back when nobody qualifies). Prizes are paid in real USDT to your wallet.

Who can join?

Anyone aged 18 or older with the MetaTrader 5 app who signs up on the website (My account), where taking part is allowed by local law. Do not take part where it is not allowed.

What can I trade?

Any symbol on the demo account, such as forex pairs, gold and indices. The bot tells you the server with your login.

When do I get my login?

At 4:25 PM UAE – in @PipClashBot if you joined on Telegram, or on the My account page if you joined on the website. It is never sent by email.

Can I see my rank during the battle?

Yes. Standings update every 10 minutes on the Live Arena page and in the bot under “My status”.

Why does my profit look different from MT5?

Your score counts only trades opened during the battle, valued at the exact end-time price.

How do I get my prize?

Prizes are added to your PipClash wallet automatically after the final result at 9:30 PM UAE. To withdraw, we check a photo ID first – the name and phone must match your account. Withdrawals are paid in USDT (BEP20).

Can I play again tomorrow?

Yes. Join again from 10:00 AM UAE and you get a new account. Battles run Monday to Friday.

I was disqualified. Can I appeal?

Message the admin through the bot during the review window (7:30 PM – 9:30 PM UAE). After the final result, decisions are final.

Still stuck? Message @PipClashBot and tap “Talk to admin”.
