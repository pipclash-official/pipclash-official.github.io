Rules and FAQ

Short version: one free entry per personone $10 entry per person, a fresh $1,000 demo account, trade only in the battle window, and the highest profit wins.

01

Entry is freeEntry is $10

There is no fee and no deposit, ever. You join on the website (My account) or through @PipClashBot on Telegram.Each entry costs $10 from your PipClash wallet. 80% of all entry fees go to that battle’s winners, 20% is our platform fee. There is no fixed prize – the more traders join, the bigger the prizes. Cancel before 10:00 AM UAE and get the fee back. Fewer than 5 traders = battle cancelled and every fee refunded.

02

One person, one entry

Each entry is tied to one phone number, on the website or on Telegram. A second entry from the same person is removed.

03

Everyone gets the same account

A fresh MT5 demo account with $1,000 and 1:500 leverage, sent to you at 4:25 PM UAE. It is used for this battle only.

04

Trade only in the battle window

Open trades only between 4:30 PM and 7:30 PM UAE. At the start your account must be untouched: $1,000 and no open trades.

05

All trades close at the end

Every position is closed automatically. Your result is calculated at the exact end-time price.

06

Highest profit wins

Rank is your profit from trades opened during the battle. Only accounts in profit can win a prize.

07

Results are checked

Every result is reviewed for two hours. The final result is published at 9:30 PM UAE and the admin’s decision is final.

08

Prizes

1st $200, 2nd $100, 3rd $50, 4th–10th $10 each. Equal profit = those prizes are shared equally. One prize per person per day. You must be 18 or older and verify a photo ID to receive a prize.

Questions

Is this real money?

No. Every account is a demo account with virtual money. You cannot lose real money. Only the prizes are real.

Who can join?

Anyone with the MetaTrader 5 app who signs up on the website (My account) or on Telegram. You must be 18 or older to receive a prize.

What can I trade?

Any symbol on the demo account, such as forex pairs, gold and indices. The bot tells you the server with your login.

When do I get my login?

At 4:25 PM UAE – in @PipClashBot if you joined on Telegram, or on the My account page if you joined on the website. It is never sent by email.

Can I see my rank during the battle?

Yes. Standings update every 10 minutes on the Live Arena page and in the bot under “My status”.

Why does my profit look different from MT5?

Your score counts only trades opened during the battle, valued at the exact end-time price.

How do I get my prize?

After the final result we contact each winner – on Telegram, or by the email on your website account – to collect payment details. We check a photo ID first – the name and phone must match your entry.

Can I play again tomorrow?

Yes. Join again from 10:00 AM UAE and you get a new account. Battles run Monday to Friday.

I was disqualified. Can I appeal?

Message the admin through the bot during the review window (7:30 PM – 9:30 PM UAE). After the final result, decisions are final.
