Terms of use

PipClash is an online MT5 demo trading competition held Monday to Friday and run by the PipClash team in the United Arab Emirates (“we”). By creating an account or joining a battle you agree to these terms and to the Rules.

Entry fee and prizes. Each entry costs the fee of the table you pick ($10, $25 or $50), paid from your PipClash wallet. 80% of a table’s entry fees are shared among its winners – traders who finish above their $1,000 start with at least 3 trades – using the prize table shown when entry closes. If only a few traders qualify, the whole pool is shared among them; if no one qualifies, 80% of every entry fee goes back to each trader’s wallet. 20% is our platform fee, from which referral and partner rewards are paid. We never promise a fixed prize – prizes depend only on how many traders join. Fees are refunded to your wallet if you cancel before you are entered, if your entry is not accepted, or if a battle is cancelled (for example when fewer than 5 traders join a table).

Wallet, deposits and withdrawals. Deposits are in USDT (TRC20 or BEP20). Withdrawals are paid in USDT on BEP20 only, after an ID check. Blockchain transfers cannot be reversed – always check the address and network. We may hold a withdrawal while we check it for fraud or a rule breach.

Demo accounts. Each player receives a demo trading account with virtual funds for one battle. The account and its virtual funds have no cash value and belong to the account provider.

Who can take part. You must be 18 or older to create an account or join a battle. One entry per person per table per day, checked by phone number. Withdrawals are paid only to the person whose name and phone match the account, checked against a photo ID.

Countries. You are responsible for making sure that taking part is lawful where you live. Do not take part where it is not allowed. We may refuse or restrict access from any country.

One person, one account. You may hold only one PipClash account. Accounts opened for the same person, on behalf of another person or with someone else’s phone or documents may be closed and their prizes withheld. Our staff and their close family may not win prizes.

Results and prizes. Results are reviewed for two hours and are final at 9:30 PM UAE; then prizes are added to winners’ wallets. We may disqualify any entry that breaks the Rules. If we later find that a result was affected by cheating, a technical error or wrong price data, we may correct it and take back a prize that was already credited. You are responsible for any taxes on prizes in your country.

Cancelled or interrupted battles. We may cancel a battle before it starts or void it if a technical failure, a server or price-feed problem or another event outside our control seriously affects it. Every entry fee for a cancelled or voided battle is returned to the wallet; no other compensation is paid.

Referrals. When someone you invite pays an entry fee, you receive 1% of it, paid from our platform fee – never from the prize pool. No reward on your own entries, on cancelled battles or on accounts created to abuse the programme. We may change or end the programme.

Suspension. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms or the Rules, uses someone else’s identity or payment, or tries to cheat. Money in the wallet that is not linked to a breach is returned after an ID check.

Privacy policy

What we collect. Your email address (or Google account email), first name, mobile number and country; your Telegram ID if you use our bot; the demo account we assign to you and its trading activity during the battle; your wallet history (deposits, entries, prizes, withdrawals and the payment or wallet addresses used); and, before a withdrawal, a photo ID.

Why. To run battles, stop duplicate entries and cheating, show rankings, keep your wallet correct and pay prizes. On public leaderboards you appear only as your first name and the last three digits of your phone number (for example “Ali-210”), with a small country flag – never the full number.
