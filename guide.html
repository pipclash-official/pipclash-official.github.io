If something goes wrong

“Invalid account” or “Authorization failed”Check the server name and copy the password again – it is case sensitive and may contain symbols like * or _.
I can’t find the serverType the full server name exactly as the bot sent it. Do not choose a different broker with a similar name.
Prices are not movingForex and gold markets are closed on weekends. Battles run Monday to Friday.
I see a different balanceYou may be logged in to an old account. Use only today’s login from the bot – every battle has a new account.
I lost my loginTap “Join” again in the bot – it sends your login again – or open My account on the website. Still stuck? Ask the admin. Never ask another player for their login.
Can I use my computer?Yes. MT5 for Windows and Mac works the same: File → Login to Trade Account, then the same three details.

Still not working? Open @PipClashBot → “Talk to admin”.
